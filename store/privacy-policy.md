# Privacy policy · collectobjects

## The short version

**Your things stay on your phone. We never see them.**

No account. No ads. No tracking. No servers of ours holding your stuff.
collectobjects is made by [your name] ("we", "us"), and this page explains
exactly what happens with your photos and words.

---

## 01 · What the app keeps, and where

- **Your objects:** the photos you take or pick, their cut-outs, names, dates and
  cities are saved **only inside the app on your phone**, in storage other apps
  can't read. We can't access them.
- **Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.** There's no copy anywhere else that
  we control.
- **Phone backups:** if backup is on for your phone, Android or iOS may include the
  app's data in your **own** Google or iCloud backup. That's managed by Google or
  Apple under your account, not by us, and you can switch it off in your phone's
  settings.

## 02 · Camera and photos

- We ask for **camera** access only so you can photograph objects. You can say no
  and still add photos from your gallery.
- When you pick a photo from your gallery, the app reads **only the photo you choose**.
- Photos are never uploaded by the app.

## 03 · Cutting out and naming your objects

Background removal and name suggestions happen **on your phone**: on Android with
Google's ML Kit (part of Google Play services), on iPhone with Apple's Vision
framework. Your photos aren't sent to us and aren't used to train anything.

On Android, ML Kit may download its models from Google, and may send Google basic
technical info about how the features perform (like device model, Android version
and app version), as described in Google's ML Kit terms. **This never includes
your photos.**

## 04 · Motion and haptics

The app reads your phone's motion sensors to tilt objects as you move and to
notice a hard shake on the home screen. These readings are used in the moment,
on your phone, and are never stored or sent anywhere. The small taps you feel use
your phone's vibration motor.

## 05 · The city (optional)

You can add the city an object was found in. The first time, the app asks for
**approximate location** (never precise). You can say no, and everything else
works the same.

- The app saves **only the city's name** (like "London") with that object, on
  your phone. No coordinates, no street, no history of where you've been.
- To turn your approximate location into a city name, your phone's geocoder
  (Google Play services on Android, Apple's on iPhone) sends that approximate
  location to Google or Apple. We never receive it.
- You can take a city off any new object with one tap, or turn location off for
  the app in your phone's settings.

## 06 · Sharing

When you share an object, the app makes a picture card on your phone and hands it
to your phone's share sheet. Where it goes from there is your choice; the app
sends nothing by itself.

## 07 · What we don't do

- No accounts or sign-in
- No ads
- No analytics or tracking
- No selling or sharing of your data, ever

## 08 · Kids

collectobjects isn't made for children under 13.
