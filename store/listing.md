## Full description (≤4000)

Memories is a quiet home for the little things in your life.

Snap a photo of an object that matters to you — your grandmother's teapot, a
ticket stub, the mug from your first apartment — and Memories cuts it out from
its background and keeps it in your personal collection, like a sticker in a
scrapbook.

KEEP THE STORY
Give each object a name and write the story behind it: where it came from, who
gave it to you, why you kept it.

FROM YOUR CAMERA OR YOUR GALLERY
Capture something new, or bring in photos you already have.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Background removal happens on your phone. Your photos are never uploaded.
• No account, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking.
• Your collection lives on your device.

CALM AND SIMPLE
No feeds, no likes, no notifications. Just your things, beautifully kept.
